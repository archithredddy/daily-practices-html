About Project

AI-Based Crop Disease Identification

This platform helps farmers identify crop diseases by uploading leaf images. AI analyzes the image, detects disease, and provides treatment suggestions.


The solution supports sustainable agriculture, reduces crop loss, and increases yield through early disease detection.
